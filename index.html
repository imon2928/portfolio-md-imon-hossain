Academic Background

Bridging Brain Science, Data Analysis & Mental Health

I am an undergraduate researcher dedicated to exploring the biological foundations of human cognition and psychological resilience. My academic focus centers on neuropsychology, psychological assessment, and quantitative empirical research.

As the Founder and Research Executive of the Dhaka College Psychology Society (DCPS), I lead interdisciplinary initiatives, run scale validation research, and design youth development programs aimed at destigmatizing mental health.

I combine statistical computation in SPSS and Python with clinical awareness tools like Psychological First Aid (PFA) to bridge research theory with actionable community health solutions.
